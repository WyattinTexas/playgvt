THE ICE CAVES · THE FEROCIOUS YETI · EVERY UNIT, EVERY DIFFICULTY

What every cave yeti, spearman, fisher, dark yeti swordsman and THE FEROCIOUS YETI does to your men on EASY, MEDIUM, HARD and MEGA, with every body's HP and head-count · read out of the live game v32.45 on Nov 14, 2026 14:06 EST · every damage number checked on the game's own machines (Chrome 401/401 · WebKit 401/401) · for Raziya

The one thing to know: no blow in the caves kills outright any more (since v29.70: no instant KO anywhere). The three that did, a swordsman's cut and THE FEROCIOUS YETI's landing (THE CHOMP, 58 px wide) on a lesser foot soldier and the falling ice on any man, now land the KO price, 31.5 · 31.5 · 31.5 · 40.5 HP whole by tier; and since v31.24 a spearman's charge and a cut on Sarge, the medic, a spared heavy or a hero land one rifleman bar, 22 · 22 · 22 · 29 HP whole. Both are worked off his bar as HP × bar ÷ his HP, with no share and no damper: a rifleman falls to any of them; every other man stands, on 15 % of his bar if the blow would have emptied a full one (THE FLOOR, since v31.00: no single hit fells a non-rifleman from a full bar), so a M. gunner keeps 0.88 of 8.75 bar hits after THE CHOMP on EASY. The jeep and the tank are still wrecked by the ice (a machine is never priced). Every other blow is bar hits off the one bar every man carries (8.8 for the men, 7.5 for the medic, 12 for the saber Sarge) × the share × the damper: a cave yeti's landing, a fisher's fish and the stomp 1 · 1 · 2 · 2 by tier, the chase's run 2, THE CHOMP on Sarge, the medic and the heavies 2 · 3 · 4 · 4, and since Nov 10 the stomp's four little hops up close (7 bar hits each). One blow reaches at most 3 · 4 · 4 · 5 men (THE CAP). And the heavies are heavy here: on top of the caves' bar (×1.25 · 1.2 · 1.15 · 1.1 by tier for every man but Sarge) the flame, the bazooka and the commando carry ×1.5 · 1.5 · 1.4 · 1.3 more, so on EASY a flame (99 HP) stands 29 landings to a rifleman's 19. King Yeti and King Rex are the exception the other way: every cave creature's blow bounces off their hide (measured: nothing lands but the ice, and the ice is the KO price on them too). Difficulty changes the bar hits (1 → 2 from HARD), THE CHOMP (2 · 3 · 4 · 4), the KO price (31.5 · 31.5 · 31.5 · 40.5), the bodies (×1 · 1.5 · 2.2 · 2.64), the head-counts, his bar (2,312 · 3,083 · 4,624 · 5,670) and the lab's level.
THE BOSS LAB fights the caves with its stock army at LEVEL 45 on EASY, 47 on MEDIUM, 48 on HARD and 50 on MEGA: Sarge with the saber, the jeep, the tank and the dogfighter (the bomber on MEGA), the jet pack on, and eight men at that rank (rifleman, M. gunner, bazooka, mortar, medic, flame, sweeper, radio man; MEGA swaps the radio man for the bomber). The grenadier, the commando and the spy are a tap away in THE PLATOON. Every "level" number on this page is that army at that tier's level. At levels 45 to 48 the men's HP is the same number (the ladder steps every tenth level); 50 is a step up.

How a hit is counted

Your men carry HP: the number on their unit sheet (a level-45 flame has 99, a rifleman 22; at level 50: 121 and 26). Their health bar is also cut into a few bar hits on EASY 8.8 for most men, 7.5 for the medic and the spy, 13.1 for the flame and the bazooka, 15 for the commando, 12 for the saber Sarge, 23 for the Claymore Sarge; every man's but Sarge's ×1.25 · 1.2 · 1.15 · 1.1 by tier in the caves). Two kinds of blow in this fight:

  • Priced blows (no KO since v29.70): a swordsman's cut and THE CHOMP on a lesser foot soldier (rifleman, M. gunner, mortar, grenadier, sweeper, radio man, spy) and the falling ice on any man take the KO price, 0.9 × the M. gunner's HP at the chart's rank for the caves' level (80 → rank 46; MEGA 120 → 69) = 31.5 · 31.5 · 31.5 · 40.5 HP; a spearman's charge and a cut on Sarge, the medic, a spared heavy or a hero take one rifleman bar (his HP at the same rank) = 22 · 22 · 22 · 29 HP (since v31.24). Either lands whole (no share, no damper) as HP × bar ÷ his HP of his bar hits. If it would empty a full bar, a rifleman falls and any other man (Sarge counts) stands on 15 % of it (THE FLOOR, since v31.00); the next hit that reaches his bar fells him. A wall still turns them: a pet's shield, Francis's block, the commando's roof, the blue wall or the dome turns the blow into bar hits (1 · 1 · 2 · 2 for the cut, 3 for a shard); the champion's grace once a life. A machine under the ice is still wrecked.
  • Bar hits (a landing, a fish, the stomp: 1 · 1 · 2 · 2; the run: 2; THE CHOMP on Sarge, the medic, a spared heavy, a machine or a hero: 2 · 3 · 4 · 4; a cut on a machine: 3; each of the stomp's little hops: 7, the heavies ×0.5 · 0.5 · 0.45 · 0.4): since v28.65 a bar hit lands 90 % + 10 % × bar ÷ HP, and since v29.17 THE DAMPER lets 50 % of every enemy blow land in the campaign and every boss fight (a priced blow is not dampened), so a level-45 rifleman takes 0.47 of his 8.8 from a cave yeti's landing (1.2 HP of 22). A hero's share is 1. A man who takes no hit for 4 s mends one bar hit, and again every 4 s. One blow (a spot inside 0.75 s) reaches at most 3 · 4 · 4 · 5 men (THE CAP, since v31.00).

Saves still roll on every bar hit: a jetting, dropping or dragged man cannot be touched, the pet shield, the deflect dome, the absorbs, and the saber Sarge's block turns a landing, a charge, a fish, a cut or THE CHOMP about half the time (measured: 2–9 of 10 landings, 3–7 of 10 cuts and 5–8 of 10 chomps turned over the four tiers and both engines). King Yeti's and King Rex's hide turns every blow of a cave creature (a thrown-snow law: the whole cave family counts as snow), the falling ice alone lands on them (the KO price). The Claymore Sarge's counted block and the riot plate keep their say (measured: the Claymore Sarge turned every cut, chomp and shard). Nothing else turns the ice but a shield or the saber's block.
