How a hit is counted

Your men carry HP: the number on their unit sheet (the flame has 55 at level 25, the rifleman 14). Their health bar is also cut into a few bar hits (6 for most level-25 men, 5 for the medic and spy, 7 for the commando, 11 for the saber Sarge). Three kinds of hit in this fight:

  • Priced bites (since v29.70, every former KO: the pounce, the big snowballs, the sprint, the leaps, King Yeti's sword, seize, wind and hop, Sergeant Snowman's ball on a light man, THEIR turret on ULTRA MEGA): 0.9 × the M. gunner's HP at the chart's rank for the castle (30 HP at rank 31, MEGA 40 at rank 54) × the castle's 0.6 · 0.6 · 0.65 · 0.9 (E · M · H · G; ULTRA MEGA × 1) × MEGA's 1.2 = 16.2 · 16.2 · 17.55 · 38.88 · 43.2 HP, as bar hits = that × bar ÷ HP. More than his bar: a rifleman falls; any other man stands on 15 % of his bar (EASY to MEGA) and the next hit fells him. A heavy (bazooka, flame, commando) reads at least twice the M. gunner's HP: the level-25 bazooka takes 1.94 of his 6 bar hits a bite on EASY.
  • Bar hits (the snowman's thrown ball): 2 bar hits a ball, ×1.2 on MEGA and ULTRA MEGA, ×1.2 on the M. gunner, landing at 50 % (the damper). Since v28.65 a bar hit lands 90 % + 10 % × bar ÷ HP, so a level-25 rifleman takes 0.94 of his 6 (2.2 HP of 14). Sergeant Snowman's ball on a heavy is 50 % of his bar at the damper's 50 %: 25 %, MEGA 30 % (bazooka · grenadier · flame · commando).
  • HP hits (THEIR turret, EASY to MEGA): a round of D lands D × 50 % (the damper) off his HP (D = 1.25 · 1.5 · 1.75 · 2, MEGA ×1.2; a heavy ×0.2): 0.625 · 0.75 · 0.875 · 1.2 HP a round on a light man. A lone man takes all 3 rounds of a burst, a burst every 0.5 s.

A man who takes no hit for 4 s mends. Saves still roll on every hit: a jetting, dropping or dragged man cannot be touched, the pet shield, the deflect dome, the absorbs, and the saber Sarge's block (50 % at rank 25) rolls twice on a priced bite, at the blow and again at the bite, so he turns about 75 % of them (measured, Chrome / WebKit: 31/32, 33/29, 31/31, 28/30, 30/31 of 40 pounces on the five tiers); THE RIOT SHIELD eats the first 5 whole and the Claymore's counted block 1 (measured: then the priced bites land until he falls). A charged commando catches a big snowball or a rolling ball and sends it back. The hero kings (King Yeti, King Rex) bounce every thrown and rolling snowball.

How this was read and checked
