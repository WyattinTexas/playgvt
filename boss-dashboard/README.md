# THE BOSS DASHBOARD · Green vs. Tan

One page for Raziya, the boss-fight designer, with the six boss fights' numbers in one place: every enemy unit, every strike and what it takes off a man on every tier, who survives at the Boss Lab's level, the level a man first survives one hit, the bosses' bars by party size, the wave sheets, what changed lately and the open questions. Wyatt's ask (10/31/2026): "let's put them all together into a dashboard, and we'll host it … so Raziya can easily access it without having to look through multiple different PDFs, and all the information is in one place."

It is served at

- https://playgvt.net/boss-dashboard/
- https://drbango.com/gvt/boss-dashboard/

and also opens from a plain copy of this folder (`index.html` from `file://`): the page loads no font, script, image or data from anywhere but this folder.

## What is on the page

- **One filter row** above everything: FIGHT (ALL or one of the six bosses), TIER (EASY · MEDIUM · HARD · MEGA · ULTRA MEGA; a tier a fight lacks is shown disabled and struck through, never hidden, and the chosen tier snaps to the nearest one the fight has), PARTY (1 · 2 · 3 · 4 · 5 · 10 commanders, for the bosses' bars) and, on ALL FIGHTS, VIEW (CARDS or BOARD). The choice lives in the address bar (`#f=ice&t=h&p=3`, `&v=board` for the board) and in the browser's own storage, so a reload keeps it and a copied link reopens it. On a phone (under 700 px wide) the row folds into one sticky strip of four selects, FIGHT · TIER · PARTY and VIEW (on a fight, GO TO a section), so the choice is always a thumb away; the chips are the row on a laptop. A chip pressed from the keyboard keeps its focus when the row re-renders, and a hidden live line reads out the new view. **Copy link to this view** puts the link on the clipboard; where the browser gives the page no clipboard (for example plain http on a LAN address) the same button shows the link in a small prompt to copy by hand. On a phone the tables' text columns (cadence, effect, notes) fold into each row's **more**, so the number cells stay compact and the words are one tap away, in a sheet that stays inside the glass.
- **ALL FIGHTS**: the six fights as cards or as one board (the fights in columns, the facts in rows, the row labels sticky on a phone): tiers with the lab's level, the tier's dials, how a hit counts in that fight (the rule's first sentences with more/less, and the mix of hit kinds on the chosen tier), the boss's bar at the chosen tier and party (both bars, named, where a boss fights on two), the units it fields, how many of the strikes that hit on the chosen tier KO a stock man in one hit (and how the fight's strikes split), the read's proof counts, and the links to the full page, the PDF and the front picture. Then the bosses' bars at the chosen tier on one scale, each boss's bars by tier on its own scale, and the strikes that KO a stock man in one hit, each chart with its table.
- **One fight**: THE RULE (how a hit is counted in that fight) and the headlines · THE TIERS (level, body HP ×, head-count ×, the boss's bar ×, bodies on the sheet, the tier's own dials, and the page's own "What changes from EASY to …" table) · THE STRIKES (every unit × every strike, the hit on every tier) · WHO SURVIVES (hits in a row to KO a fresh man at the lab's level; a + in a cell's corner = a save ate some first, the riot shield's 5 or the Claymore's counted block, so the number is every hit that reaches him, the words in the cell's tooltip; the heat grid walks with the arrow keys) · THE LEVEL A MAN FIRST SURVIVES ONE HIT · THE BODIES (HP as spawned and head-count on every tier) · THE BOSS (every move with its hit on every tier, the fight page's own rows beside them — the clocks, the gates, the walk, the bar, the toy box, marked "the page" — the bar by party size with a caption saying which series it is, and where THE BOSS LAB's own party slider reads another number (the Pillow Fields) that series as its own table, the acts' bosses' bars, and a unit of its own bar by party size: the Ice Castle's sergeants, the Pillow Fields' cavalryman) · MORE FROM THE PAGE (the page's own tables the sheet has no column for: the Ice Castle's turrets, the Pillow Fields' cavalry line and funnel, the hideout's hits in HP) · THE WAVES · WHAT CHANGED · the 🟨 QUESTIONS · the kit's notes · SOURCES (the kit's read clock and the page's own stamp, both in New York time, EDT or EST by the instant).
- **Three kinds of hit**, and the page never adds one to another: **HP** takes HP off the man's sheet (in King Rex's lair these grow with the tier); **bar hits** take a share of his health bar (90 % + 10 % × bar ÷ HP of the weight lands); **KO** takes him out in one, whatever his HP. Each fight's rule says which law it follows: no hit is an instant KO since Nov 3 (a former KO is a priced bite of HP, landing whole) and since Nov 6 no single hit fells a man other than a rifleman from a full bar; the lair mixes HP, priced bites, bar hits and one KO (the triceratops' charge on a light man, Wyatt's word of Nov 13), the hideout is bar hits (the giant rattlesnake's swallow takes a man's whole bar left), the palace bar hits with two priced HP bites (the ram, her face shot) and two hits that fell (her auto attack on a man on foot, her tongue's jump-back crit), the Ice Castle, the Pillow Fields and the Ice Caves priced bites with a few bar hits.
- Dark mode follows the system (or the Theme control), with its own colour steps; every chart has a table twin; a tooltip closes with Escape and stays while the pointer rests on it; every control is reachable by keyboard, and a chart is one Tab stop whose marks the arrow keys walk (Home and End to the ends).

Two Boss Lab fights have no breakdown yet and get one quiet line: THE SKY FORTRESS · THE BUFFALO BILL SHOWDOWN (THE ICE CAVES joined as the sixth on Nov 1, 2026).

- **THE ARMY** (the last chip on the FIGHT row; CARD-ARM, Nov 2, 2026, Wyatt's word: "Please add in the player unit scaling as well"): the player's units level by level, every man, Sarge on each of his irons and the heroes from level 0 to 100. THE RULE (the ladder: the units, a man's hit ×, a hero's hit ×, Sarge's crit odds) · AT LEVEL n (a slider and a box for the level; the fights' lab levels on the chosen tier as one-tap chips; HP, the bar, the blows of one bar hit a man stands at the tier's damper, the hit, a crit, per second, crit odds, reach) · BY LEVEL (HP, THE BAR, THE HIT or PER SECOND at 0 · 1 · 5 · 10 … 100, a curve per row with the chosen level and the fights' lab levels marked) · SARGE'S IRONS (and the look page with every number, iron by iron) · THE FIGHTS' LEVELS (tap a level to read the army there) · the questions. The link remembers the level (`l`) and what the table shows (`m`).

## What is in this folder

- `index.html` the page (markup, style and a small script; no framework).
- `data.js` the six data files' numbers and words inlined as `window.BOSS_DATA` (so the page opens from `file://`); `art.js` the units' and men's pictures as `window.BOSS_ART`, loaded after the numbers have rendered (a monogram tile stands in until a picture arrives).
- `data/<key>.json` one file per fight in the shape `design/boss-dashboard-1031/SCHEMA.md` (gvt-boss-breakdown/1): `bone` King Rex's lair · `croak` Quick Draw Croak's hideout (King Croak until v31.31) · `ice` the Ice Castle (King Yeti) · `palace` Princess Croak's palace · `fields` the Pillow Fields (Emperor Bear) · `caves` the Ice Caves (THE FEROCIOUS YETI).
- `data/army.json` THE ARMY: the player's units at every level 0 to 100 (schema gvt-army-scale/1, in the same SCHEMA.md); folded into `data.js` as `BOSS_DATA.army`.
- `pages/` each fight's full breakdown as a one-file HTML page, a Letter PDF and a front PNG; and SARGE'S IRONS (`GVT-Sarge-Irons-v29.25.*`), every damage number of his irons, level by level.
- `README.md` this file.

No file here carries a key, a token, an e-mail address, a home path, a machine name or a chat id; nothing on the page is typed by hand.

## How to rebuild

All commands run from the repo root; `NODE_PATH=/opt/homebrew/lib/node_modules` for the Node rigs, Chrome for Testing as the only Chrome, every browser muted, zero game bytes.

1. **A fight's kit** (only when the game changed): inside its folder, the read, the proof and the page, for example King Yeti's
   `node kit/readYeti.mjs chrome <index.html> && node kit/readYeti.mjs webkit <index.html> && node kit/proveYeti.mjs chrome <index.html> && node kit/proveYeti.mjs webkit <index.html> && python3 kit/build.py && node kit/render.mjs <html> <png> <front.png> <pdf>`
   (King Rex: `readLair.mjs` / `proveLair.mjs`; King Croak: `readHideout.mjs` / `proveHideout.mjs`; Princess Croak: `readPalace.mjs` / `provePalace.mjs`; Emperor Bear: `readBear.mjs` / `proveBear.mjs`; the Ice Caves: `readCaves.mjs` / `proveCaves.mjs`, which read HEAD's own client with `git show` and take no path; each record's "The kit" section has the exact line). The two new kits' `build.py` stamp their pages with the zone they were built in (for example `… on Nov 1, 2026 03:48 EST`); the three landed kits still write a bare wall-clock, which `page_tables.py` accepts except inside the fall-back hour, where it refuses and asks for a zoned re-render.
   **The five fights in one go** (Nov 14, 2026, CARD-BDB-KO: the lair, the hideout, the castle, the Pillow Fields, the caves; the palace and THE ARMY apart): pin the client once (`git show HEAD:index.html > <scratch>/index-pinned.html`) and `bash design/boss-dashboard-ko-1114/land/rerun_ko.sh <scratch>/index-pinned.html` runs every read and proof on both engines, the rasters, the five pages and files, the validation, the build and the check below in this order (about 9 minutes of browser time), its log in `design/boss-dashboard-ko-1114/land/logs/rerun.log`.
2. **The pictures over the 60 KB cap** (a sprite the game encodes bigger than the schema allows is drawn to a PNG): `node design/boss-dashboard-1031/kit/raster_art.mjs` → `design/boss-dashboard-1031/data/art-raster.json` and the contact sheet `proof/art-rasters.png`. It needs every kit's read on ONE client: since Nov 14, 2026 (CARD-BDB-KO) the five reads it takes (the lair, the hideout, the castle, the Pillow Fields; the caves' sprites all fit the cap) stand on one client again and the rasters were drawn there; the palace draws its own since Nov 13 (`design/boss-princess-unit-breakdown-1031/kit/rasterPalace.mjs`) and is left out. An exporter accepts a raster only from its read's client or from sprites byte-identical to it (checked on every run).
3. **The exporters** write `boss-dashboard/data/<key>.json` and copy the fight's page, PDF and front PNG into `boss-dashboard/pages/`, lift the page's own extra tables with `design/boss-dashboard-1031/kit/page_tables.py` (`extraTables`), then cross-check the JSON against the landed page:
   `python3 -I design/boss-dashboard-1031/kit/export_bone.py` · `python3 -I design/boss-dashboard-1031/kit/export_croak.py` · `python3 -I design/boss-dashboard-1031/kit/export_ice.py` · `python3 design/boss-princess-unit-breakdown-1031/kit/export_dash.py` · `python3 design/boss-emperor-unit-breakdown-1031/kit/export_dash.py` · `python3 design/boss-caves-unit-breakdown-1101/kit/export_dash.py`
   - **THE KO PASS is retired** (Nov 14, 2026, CARD-BDB-KO): its Nov 1 proposal (a number for every one-hit KO) became the game's own law on Nov 3 (NO INSTANT KO: a former KO takes 0.9 × the M. gunner's sheet HP at the chart's rank, landing whole) under the full-bar floor of Nov 6, and the six files carry the priced bites the game plays. It is no longer run: `design/boss-ko-pass-1101/kit/ko_pass.py` prints why and writes nothing (`--force-retired` runs it as it was, history only).
4. **Validate** each file against the schema: `python3 -I design/boss-dashboard-1031/kit/validate.py boss-dashboard/data/<key>.json`
5. **Build the page**: `python3 -I design/boss-dashboard-1031/kit/build_dash.py` → `boss-dashboard/index.html` + `boss-dashboard/data.js` + `boss-dashboard/art.js` (deterministic; the only clocks on the page are the files' own `readAt` and `builtAt`, shown in New York time by the zone's own rules).
