# GVT — Green vs. Tan

A toy-soldier war on the bedroom carpet. Plays in any browser at **[playgvt.net](https://playgvt.net)**.

- **Campaign** — endless levels, bosses every third, Sarge's arsenal (shotgun / sniper / lightsaber).
- **Battlefield** — a two-sided lane-pusher against the daily rival army. Drop men on your half,
  march across, smash more of their toy box than they smash of yours before 1:30 is up.
- **Invasions** — challenge any real player on the world rankings and raid their room.

Single self-contained `index.html`. No build step, no dependencies.
