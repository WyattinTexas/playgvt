SAME DICE (default OFF = REAL DICE, the law): the next run's dice are seeded before the game boots and re-seeded at every level start and wave push, so a run after a change meets the same waves as the run before, and a difference in feel is the change, not the luck. Honest about what it is: the same waves, not a frame-identical battle (your taps and the fight inside a wave spend the dice their own way). The header and every log row of a seeded run name the seed (SAME DICE 4211); a bookmark returned under SAME DICE re-seeds at the return and says so.

Both OFF = the phone's rhythm: six silent games and the cliff (no card until campaign 5 is beaten), the birthday gate at the first room visit, the first AD PLACEMENT card at the exit of game 8 or 9, then every two or three games. EVERY SEAT: a card at every exit where a phone could break (the cradle and the cliff lifted, the first game not free, the clock every game; the minute gap and the sitting cap stand). 4 · About this bench

  • Public. This page is playgvt.net/bench/. Open it on the Go Machine, on the phone, anywhere. It plays the DEPLOYED build, the same bytes the phones and the web play; when a new build ships, the UPDATE NOW chip appears in a quiet room, exactly as on the web. The loop: a card ships, the mirror updates, tap RESET, play.
  • Never play the real web game in this browser. The bench and the real web game at playgvt.net share one browser's save, and RESET wipes it. Play the real game on the phone apps.
  • HQ never hears of the bench commander. Every call to HQ is answered on this page, in the page's own bytes; nothing syncs. RANKINGS shows the real board with no YOU row (a real fresh player's row lands under the board's eight rows after the first sync).
  • CHOOSE YOUR NAME still lays over the board on the first RANKINGS visit, prefilled with the minted callsign, and SAVE keeps it locally with nobody at HQ to veto it. Sarge's one safety line fires at the first multiplayer door or the first RANKINGS visit, once ever per throwaway.
  • The multiplayer, rated, away, event and challenge doors are quiet or say HQ is unreachable. No Apple or Google link, no rename from HQ. The store is a page with no bridge (never faked, never priced); the run log records any store surface as a web fall-through.
  • AD PLACEMENT. The bench stands in as the phone's ad provider: wherever a phone player would sit through a real ad, a full-screen card reading AD PLACEMENT with the seat's name under it (INTERSTITIAL · GAME 8 · the verdict exit, or REWARDED · THE TV); a tap anywhere goes on, the X from frame one. Nothing is served. The GAME decides when a break is due (the six-game cradle, the campaign-5 cliff, the two-to-three clock, the minute gap, the sitting cap); the bench only paints, and every exit gets a log row saying whether a card showed and, if not, why. On a true reset the first few levels never meet a card: that is the design. The TV asks first in the game's own words (ROLL IT!), then the card: a tap finishes it and the set pays +30 stars once a day; the X pays nothing and keeps the day.
  • The birthday gate asks once per reset at the first room visit, as on a phone with the new shell; THAT'S ME with a 13+ date arms the provider, the X skips it (zero ads this sitting, it asks again next boot). The log records the track, never the date. After a card, the room shows the 3-second SEE A BAD AD? REPORT IT chip, a real link to the support page, left live.
  • EVERY SEAT and TRACK PRE-ANSWERED are the two switches above and on the DIALS page in the game; the run header names them. RETREAT (the DIALS page) forfeits the running battle through the game's own loss door, so the weak player's branches (NOT THIS TIME, TRY AGAIN, quiet mercy after two straight losses) run for real without an hour of deliberate losing; on L1 it reads THE TAN RETREAT?! by the game's own rule.
  • THE LEDGER (the LEDGER button, the DIALS page, the strip's road line, COPY RUN's road table) reads the reward road off the game's own tables (never typed into the bench: TREASURE_ROAD, GIFT_ROAD, DECOR_EARN, the room gifts, the park gifts, the lens's two doors, the weapon rites, the pacing rules) and marks every prize OWED, HELD (the game's own pacing, with the reason: DINO PARK owns the moment, one paced beat a session, one card at a time), GRANTED, ANNOUNCED (the ticket on the win face, the card at home: two seats, both stamped) and TAKEN (your tap). MISSING fires only when a landing's line ended without a prize the road owed; EARLY when a prize arrived below its rung; TAXED on a replayed rung; boot-owed after a return. Beside every rung stands the DESIGN road the docs intend: a mismatch prints DRIFT, never MISSING.
  • THE HIDDEN HAND. On every launch and verdict a HAND row says in plain words which hand the game played: the first battle always a victory, quiet mercy after two straight losses, the box alarm on rungs 1 to 5 and the proven player's earlier bite, the casualty refund and Sarge's ten seconds, the battleground's rookie games and the director, the whispers. The hard-bot gate, Nemesis and MOM at the door are out of the first levels' window by design and the boot row says so. A WEAK HAND preset is not shipped: RETREAT walks the real road to the same place.
