Corkscrew Games

Green vs Tan is a tower defense game fought with classic plastic army men across the carpet of a kid's bedroom. Hold the toy box, place your riflemen, and stop the Tan advance. Play at playgvt.net or in the app editions.

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. A commander is created for you the first time you play, and it persists on your device. Sign in with Apple is offered only if you want the same army on more than one device.

How do I play with a friend? Tap CO-OP on the title screen. One of you hosts and reads out the room code; the others join with it. Up to ten commanders can defend the same carpet.

How do I get better troops? Win battles to earn dog tags and stars, then spend stars at Sarge's Surplus. Your rank climbs as you fight — a hundred ranks, from Recruit up.

The Tan are too tough. Reposition between waves — every soldier can be dragged during the twenty-second window after a wave clears. Tap a downed man to stand him back up.

My stars or rank look wrong. Email us with your commander name and what you expected — we'll look at the record.
